# 5 YouTube video ideas — business + AI

Context used: channel covers Karolis's business (Flow Bound Agency) and how he uses AI in it.
`notes/00-about-me.md` doesn't yet say what the agency actually sells or who buys it — these
ideas are written to work regardless of that, but they'd get sharper titles/hooks once that's
filled in.

## 1. "I Run My Agency's Ops on AI — Full Stack Walkthrough"
Screen-recorded tour of the actual tools and workflows (Claude, this repo's issue queue,
whatever else is live) — what each one does, what it replaced, what it costs. Behind-the-scenes
format performs well because it's hard to fake and viewers can copy it directly.

## 2. "How Many Hours AI Actually Saves Me Per Week (Tracked for 30 Days)"
Log real time saved/spent across a month, show the raw numbers, be honest about where AI
made things slower. Data-driven honesty beats hype in this niche — builds trust fast.

## 3. "5 AI Tools I Use to Run the Agency (and 3 I Dropped)"
Half win, half fail. The "dropped" tools are the more interesting half — specifics on why they
didn't work stick better than another "best AI tools" list.

## 4. "I Let AI Run My Business for a Day"
Constrained experiment: AI handles as much as possible for one full day (client comms drafts,
scheduling, research, this repo's async task queue) while Karolis just approves/rejects.
Show what broke and what it got right. Strong thumbnail/title, easy to script.

## 5. "The AI Workflow That Actually Gets Me Client Work"
Whatever part of the funnel AI touches — outreach drafts, proposal generation, research on
prospects — walked through end to end with a real (or realistic) example. This is the one to
customize most once the actual offer/ICP is confirmed, since "gets me clients" only lands if
viewers can see it's a real service being sold.
